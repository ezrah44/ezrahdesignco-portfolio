What I Did

Identity

Ax Performance is Axel Sanderson’s strength and conditioning practice. He coaches one on one, in small groups and with whole teams. Axel coaches by the evidence, not the hype, and his brand needed to look as sharp as his programming, not like every other gym logo.

The mark is an A and an X sharing the same strokes, set on a forward lean. Volt green on charcoal gives it energy in a dark gym, and it runs through everything Axel puts in a client’s hands, from business cards and bottles to his Instagram.
