Kirk’s Bike Shop, building a modern bike shop with a vintage soul.

What I Did

Identity

Kirk’s the guy who got me into biking, and for years we’ve joked about running a bike shop together. This is that shop: the logo, mockups, and a few social ads, all built for Kirk.

Most bike shops right now feel pretty corporate and modernized, so I wanted to go in a direction that aligned with Kirk’s vintage style and retro feel while still feeling new and fresh. Oh, and the orange is on purpose too. 👨🏻‍🦰 (Kirk).
